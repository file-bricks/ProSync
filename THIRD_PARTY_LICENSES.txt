## Architecture & Security Invariants

- INV-LOCAL-01: 100% Local-First & Zero Egress (zero telemetry, zero phone-home calls)
- INV-RUNAS-02: Unprivileged User Mode (RunAsInvoker Non-Elevation, zero UAC required)
- INV-WAL-03: SQLite WAL Crash Safety (automated pre-sync checkpoint PRAGMA wal_checkpoint(TRUNCATE))
- INV-INTEG-04: Atomic File Copy & Crash Resilience (.tmp staging prior to atomic rename)
- INV-SCHED-05: DST-Aware Canonical Scheduling (IANA timezones without catch-up cascades)
- INV-PWA-06: Redacted Offline Companion Export (prosync-profile-v1.json credential scrubbing)
- INV-PLAT-07: Cross-Platform Parity (Linux & macOS standardized 8-point smoke contract)
- INV-STORE-08: Windows Store / MSIX Staging (AppxManifest Geiger.ProSync & Policy 10.1.3)
- INV-DOCS-09: Trilingual 18-Point Quick Navigation Parity (EN/DE/ES sec-01..sec-18 dual anchors)
- INV-SLA-10: Open Source Governance & SLA (MIT, § 521 BGB Gefälligkeitsrecht, 48h response / 5d triage)
